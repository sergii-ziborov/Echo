## Current purchase and planned transition

The public App Store 1.0.0 is still one paid download without advertising or in-app purchases. The 1.1.0 beta prepares a free transition; testing does not change the public price or grant an additional production purchase.

After the transition is enabled, all existing worlds, modes, research and Archive content remain available with normal progression. One non-consumable Remove Ads purchase disables all in-game advertising. The App Store supplies the price and currency. There is no subscription, paid life, energy timer or purchase of gameplay power. Original acquisitions in the approved protected period retain advertising-free access without another payment.

Settings → Ads and Purchases → Restore Purchases checks the IAP and original app acquisition. Purchase/restoration may need internet; core gameplay works offline. Family Sharing is not promised for Remove Ads. A verified refund may end the IAP right, while an independently verified original-owner right remains protected.

## Advertising and progress

When enabled, advertising is limited to suitable solo campaign result transitions. The first five maps, Retry/Rewind, deaths, knowledge discoveries and Watch play remain protected. An unavailable advertisement never blocks progression. Rewarded continuation is not part of R1.

Game progress is local. Reset or removal may delete it; restoring purchases does not synchronize campaign progress. Reset keeps purchase evidence separate. Features and balance may change in updates.
