## Access and Remove Ads

ECHO is free to download after the confirmed free transition. All existing worlds, modes, research and knowledge remain available with normal progression. There is no subscription, paid life/energy system or purchase of gameplay power.

One non-consumable Remove Ads purchase disables all advertising. The App Store displays the actual localized price. Original paid owners keep ad-free access without another payment. Settings → Ads and Purchases → Restore Purchases checks both rights. Purchasing or first restoration may require internet; ordinary gameplay works offline. Family access is promised only where the actual product supports it and StoreKit verifies it.

The first five maps, deaths, Retry/Rewind, discoveries, region ends and Watch sessions are protected. An unavailable ad never blocks the next level. Rewarded rescue is not part of this release.
