# ECHO Privacy Policy

## Your progress and purchases

ECHO has no developer account. Campaign progress, research, inventory, recovered records and settings remain on your device. Apple processes purchases. StoreKit verifies original acquisition and Remove Ads rights; the app keeps minimal verification references locally in device-only Keychain. The developer does not receive your card details or Apple ID. Resetting game progress does not reset purchase rights. Core gameplay works offline; purchases and restoration may require internet.

## Advertising and choices

Original paid owners and players with verified Remove Ads rights are protected from advertising. While ownership is uncertain, no ads are shown. Eligible free players may encounter rare interstitials at explicit Next transitions after successful solo campaign results. The first five maps, deaths, Retry, Rewind, discoveries, region ends, Daily, Deep Time, Watch and watch-controlled sessions are protected. There are no rewarded, banner or launch ads in R1.

The iOS advertising provider is Google Mobile Ads with Google's UMP privacy-choice tools. Initialization and ad requests require eligible rights, release policy and privacy status. The request configuration disables publisher first-party ID and personalization and uses restrictive content/age treatment. ECHO does not request IDFA. This does not mean the SDK collects no information. Depending on actual SDK operation, Google may process IP/network and approximate location inferred from IP, device information/identifiers, crash/performance diagnostics, ad activity and interactions for advertising and measurement. See [Google's SDK disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Settings → Ads and Purchases provides the supplier's privacy options when required and a user-initiated inappropriate-ad report. Remove Ads stops ECHO's future ad requests/shows. It does not erase data already transmitted or unload a running third-party SDK; privacy choices remain reachable afterward. With no permitted ad or no network, gameplay continues.

## Apple and technical services

Optional Game Center uses Apple's service for ratings and achievements. WatchConnectivity exchanges progress and controls between paired devices; the Watch app contains no ad SDK. No developer analytics service or extra gameplay telemetry is added.

A configured signed-policy endpoint may receive ordinary HTTPS request metadata such as IP and user agent; no account/device ID or route is added to its request. Hosting and advertising providers handle technical data under their policies. The actual endpoint, retention and production data disclosures must be confirmed in the release privacy audit before enabling live advertising.

## Contact and reports

Bug reports open the player's mail app with app/device/system details. Ad reports contain app version, placement, time and an optional limited diagnostic reference. Neither contains raw receipts, signed transactions, advertising IDs, routes or Archive answers. Nothing is sent unless the player sends it.
