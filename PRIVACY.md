# Privacy Policy — ECHO

Last updated: 27 September 2026

ECHO does not collect personal data and does not require an account.

## Data stored on the device

Stars, shards, inventory, research, last map, and audio or haptic preferences are stored only on this device using Apple’s standard `UserDefaults`.

With a paired Apple Watch, wrist clears and relics pass between your iPhone and the watch through Apple’s WatchConnectivity, and a run steered from the watch sends its controls the same way. That traffic stays between your own devices; nothing goes to the developer.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on the device, the app sends your two ratings (iPhone and Apple Watch) to its leaderboards and your achievement progress to Apple's Game Center. Game Center is Apple's service and works under Apple's privacy policy; the developer does not receive your Apple ID or contact details. Signing in is optional, and the game plays the same without it.

## Bug reports

**Settings → Report a bug** opens your mail app with a message to the developer that already names the app version, device model and system version. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the message is used only to answer you and fix the problem.

## What the app does not do

The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, its own accounts, or network calls required to play.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the App Store listing and this file will be updated together.
