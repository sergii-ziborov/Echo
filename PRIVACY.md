# Privacy Policy — ECHO

Last updated: 20 September 2026

ECHO does not collect personal data and does not require an account.

## Data stored on the device

Stars, shards, inventory, research, last map, and audio or haptic preferences are stored only on this device using Apple’s standard `UserDefaults`.

## What the app does not do

The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or network calls required to play.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the App Store listing and this file will be updated together.
