## Free transition and Remove Ads

After the owner-approved transition, downloading ECHO is free. All existing campaign regions, modes, research and knowledge remain available with normal progression unlocks. No subscription, paid lives, energy, paid waiting or sale of gameplay power is introduced.

One non-consumable Remove Ads purchase disables all in-game advertising from ECHO. The App Store supplies the actual price/currency. Original acquisitions in the approved protected period keep ad-free access without another payment. Settings → Ads and Purchases → Restore Purchases checks both sources. Internet may be required for purchasing or initial restoration on another device; ordinary gameplay remains available offline. No Family Sharing promise is made unless the product is actually configured for it and StoreKit verifies the right.

Advertising, when enabled, is limited to suitable solo-campaign result transitions. Retry, Rewind, the first five campaign maps, discoveries and Watch play remain protected. An unavailable ad never blocks progression. Rewarded continuation is not part of R1.

The earlier paid release remains without advertising and without a second purchase offer while the price transition is incomplete. This document is not a claim that a production IAP has already been configured or approved.

## Progress and rights

Game progress is local. Reset or removal may delete local game data; purchase restoration is separate and does not synchronize campaign progress. Reset does not intentionally remove purchase evidence. A verified refund/revocation of Remove Ads may end that IAP right, while an independently verified original-owner right remains protected. Consumer rights and Apple's applicable purchase/refund rules remain unchanged.
