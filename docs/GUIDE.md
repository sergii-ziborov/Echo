# ECHO player guide

ECHO is a route-planning survival game. Your orb is the **bright cyan core**; violet orbs are your recorded past. Collect every required spark, then reach the illuminated exit without touching an Echo or a lethal hazard. When you lift your finger, the orb pulses so you can find it again.

## Why you are out here

The orb is the Signal, the last light of the Lighthouse, sent down the Fold Road after time broke. Sparks are loose seconds of the present; collecting them opens the exit, a fold into the next place. Each act is a different region of space, so its sky, walls and hazards change with it. The Archive's **Story** page has the full account, and every map's place and log appear in the Atlas and on the pause card.

## Your first run

1. Drag anywhere on the arena to steer. The orb seeks your finger; releasing lets it coast.
2. Watch the Echo clock near the starting beacon. At zero, a violet copy begins replaying the route you just took. The clock is not a level timeout.
3. Leave open lanes for your future self. Broad loops are safer than crossing the same point repeatedly.
4. Double-tap the arena to dash. It has its own cooldown; it does **not** spend a Rewind charge.
5. Collect all gold sparks and enter the active exit. Timed cyan crystals are optional: reach them before their ring closes for extra research points and Freeze time. They remain collectible afterward, without that timing bonus.

If you crash, the fracture screen offers **Rewind** when a charge is available. It sends you back along your recent route, but the discarded branch remains as a temporary dangerous ghost. Restart is always available.

## Reading the HUD

The top counters show sparks collected, Echoes active versus the map's limit, and Rewind charges. The Pause button opens restart, settings and menu actions. Ability buttons sit at the bottom; each shows its remaining charges and cooldown. A shield status badge indicates a hit it can still absorb.

The Echo clock counts down to another replay, while a crystal's own ring counts down only its **bonus**. Freeze pauses hostile motion, Echo playback and relevant countdowns, including brittle asteroid fracture clocks. When the Echo limit is reached, its spawn clock disappears.

## The Temporal Lab

The Lab has two tabs:

- **Skills**: unlock available abilities, buy charges, and choose the limited loadout that appears during a run. You can own more abilities than you can equip.
- **Upgrades**: permanently improve your orb and abilities in the 24-node Timeline Matrix. Motion, Loadout and Time are three related branches.

To research a node, tap it, read the current and next-rank effect, and check the listed prerequisites. Every listed prerequisite must be met at its stated rank; an outlined locked node cannot be bought yet. When eligible and you have enough research points, press **Upgrade** in the detail panel. The glowing connection shows a reachable route, not an automatic unlock. The Slot Matrix increases available ability slots; Deep Reserves increases charges; Fast Cycle reduces cooldowns. Early Vector Drive ranks open access to several later branches.

Earn research points by clearing maps, pursuing the three optional Temporal Seals on each map, collecting timed crystals and chaining nearby sparks for Resonance. Daily Rift gives its point reward on the first clear of the day.

## Abilities and hazards

Freeze stops moving hazards and their clocks while you keep moving. Surge accelerates the orb. Shield absorbs a hit. Magnet draws sparks. Phase crosses Echoes and other eligible hazards. Pulse and Shift postpone the next Echo; Anchor slows the hostile world; Repulse pushes nearby threats; Prism protects against lasers; Blink jumps forward across danger. The Lab and in-game Wiki show each ability's exact timing and recommended use.

Asteroids have distinct material shells and faint movement trails. Their routes vary by map: ricochets, patrols, paired orbits, and large stationary cores with three smaller orbiters. Cryo Ice, Chrono Crystal and Basalt crack after wall impacts and may eventually break; Void Alloy does not. Later acts add Magma Cores, Meteoric Iron, Hollow Geodes and Comet Frost, each with its own look and toughness. Lasers telegraph before firing. Black holes pull toward a lethal core. Rifts can stop time, collapse, warp space or lead into the faster Candy Timeline. These systems arrive gradually across the atlas, so inspect a region and its map before entering.

## Atlas, difficulty and saves

The Timeline Atlas contains **77 maps in 11 seven-map regions**. Swipe between regions or tap a region icon, then tap a map node to inspect it. Locked maps are shown for orientation; clear the preceding route to progress. Each map has three optional seals: Clear, Control and Paradox. Finishing all 77 starts a harder named difficulty cycle with its own seal and record set, while completed cycles remain recorded.

Progress is stored on the device. Settings lets you change sound volume, haptics and replay preferences. **Reset progress** requires confirmation and erases maps, difficulty, records, points, inventory and research while preserving sound and haptic preferences.

For a quick reminder inside the game, open **Wiki** from the home screen. The four-beat Field Training tutorial is available when starting a new timeline.
