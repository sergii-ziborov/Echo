# ECHO — memory, evidence and the next turn

The Keepers built the Fold Road to carry state records between distant stations. The Lighthouse supplies a reference so the stations can compare those records. During the Break, a recovery controller began treating its last confirmed state as an instruction to repeat it. Its measurements could no longer correct its assumptions.

The Signal carries new evidence through that damaged system. Its echoes repeat measured movement, while the player can choose a different next turn. Sparks carry local state records. The Archive preserves voices and discoveries, including disagreement and uncertainty. Recovery succeeds when the network can retain its past without requiring every future state to reproduce it.

This is the story's common question: **what should memory preserve, and when should judgment choose a new action?** Science tests explanations of the network; ethics asks whom recovery serves. The philosophers offer distinct perspectives, not one doctrine or a physical proof of the fiction. All in-game interpretations are original prose, not quotations. No therapeutic claim is made.

The Ashcrown stations retain observations of different ages. Region order is a route through the recovery network, not a chronological stellar-collapse animation. The field wells have designed ranges and lethal cores; they are not a collection of astrophysical black holes. Coordinate transformations do not teleport objects. Rewind restores a fictional local cell snapshot rather than the historical universe. Camus's Sisyphus does not accumulate a productive archive; retained records are ECHO's own addition to that comparison.

## Mechanics remain in the world

- Lab: Keeper modules calibrate the Signal. Research retains calibration across attempts. Charges power activations; cooldown is recovery time. This changes explanatory copy, not the economy or effects.
- Daily Rift: a repeated diagnostic with a changed arrangement of sparks.
- Deep Time: a separate procedural expedition beyond the surveyed route.
- Watch: the optional Keeper Chronometer, with 36 rooms; the same device can act as a remote for a phone run. These are different play paths.
- Discoveries: recovered through progression and presented after attempts or when the player opens the Archive. Reading does not become a paid or advertising gate.

The 77 authored map fragments already connect their actual hazards to their locations. Their titles, mechanical tips, stable `awakening-N` identities, unlock rules and rewards are retained. This update expands all eleven region interpretations, corrects science notes and develops nine recovered character records. It does not replace the campaign or claim new experiments exist.
